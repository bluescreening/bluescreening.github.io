This is... exactly what it says on the tin. A list of all the CDs I own, in alphabetical order by artist, with a little description of why I own them. Maybe you will find some music you've never heard but love! Or maybe you think I'm stupid and possibly insane. Regardless, it is here. Enjoy.![Post Human: Survival Horror](/assets/images/blog-pics/post-human.jpg)

_10,000 Gecs_ by 100 Gecs - Hyperpop/pop punk/ska - Got me through sixth form.

_Licensed to Ill_ by Beastie Boys - Rap rock/hip-hop - My mum had it. I don't even like the album that much...

_Atomic: The Very Best of Blondie_ by Blondie - New wave - I am genetically predisposed to new wave.

_Parklife_ by Blur - Britpop - It fills me with a sense of enormous wellbeing.

_Changesbowie_ by David Bowie - Glam rock - I am not immune to David Bowie.

_Post Human: Survival Horror_ by Bring Me The Horizon - Alt metal/nu metal/industrial - Absolute killer headbanging material.

_International Velvet_ by Catatonia - Alt rock - Catatonia's greatest album and a pandemic favourite.![International Velvet](/assets/images/blog-pics/International_Velvet.jpg)

_Equally Cursed and Blessed_ by Catatonia - Rock - Second favourite Catatonia album, features Londinium (banger).

_Strange Glue_ by Catatonia - Alt rock - The B-side is _That's All Folks_ which is my favourite Catatonia song, and it wasn't available on Spotify until recently so I was thrilled to find this single.

_Discovery_ by Daft Punk - French house/electro-funk - Uncontroversially one of the best albums ever, and I am a huge fan of _Interstella 555: The 5story of the 5ecret 5tar 5ystem_ (have seen it in cinemas!).

_Human After All_ by Daft Punk - Dance-rock - _Robot Rock_ is here.

_Maggot_ by Dazey and the Scouts - Riot grrrl/punk rock/queercore - **My favourite album of all time.**

_...For the Whole World to See_ by Death - Punk rock - A beautiful album and a piece of punk history.![Maggot](/assets/images/blog-pics/Maggot.jpg)

_Planet Her_ by Doja Cat - Pop/hip-hop/R&B - Nice background music for hangouts.

_Deleted Scenes from the Cutting Room Floor_ by Caro Emerald - Electro-swing - I was a massive electro-swing fan as a kid so picked this up for nostalgia reasons.

_The Secret to Life_ by Fizz - Indie pop - Got it for Orla Gartland, but a couple of really good songs on here esp. _High in Brighton_.

_Yoshimi Battles the Pink Robots_ by The Flaming Lips - Neo-psychedelia/space rock - Historic, cool as hell and pleasant to listen to.

_The Very Best of Fleetwood Mac_ by Fleetwood Mac - Rock - Is there any question as to why Fleetwood Mac belongs on every CD rack in the world? They lasted for four decades. Objectively they are good.

_Woman on the Internet_ by Orla Gartland - Indie pop - Formative album for me, extremely yummy stuff. Caught covid at the show, still worth it.![Woman On The Internet](/assets/images/blog-pics/woman-on-the-internet.jpg)

_Live Through This_ by Hole - Alt rock/grunge/punk rock - I was recommended this by my neglectful ex-girlfriend which I believe is the intended listening experience.

_Hozier_ by Hozier - Blues/soul/indie rock - Legendary charity shop pull, truly excellent album and great background music.

_Razzmatazz_ by I Don't Know How But They Found Me - Indie pop/new wave/pop rock - Another sixth form obsession turned pretty decent album, well worth a listen.

_Slippery When Wet_ by Bon Jovi - Glam metal - Truly exceptional album, formative to my music taste.

_Diva_ by Annie Lennox - Pop/soul - Picked this up in a charity shop for _Walking on Broken Glass_.

_Special_ by Lizzo - Pop/funk/disco - Really solid pop album.

_Galore_ by Kirsty MacColl - Only purchased for _There's a Guy Works Down the Chip Shop Swears He's Elvis_ but I have to put some respect on her name because she died heroically saving her son from a speedboat accident.![Black Holes and Revelations](/assets/images/blog-pics/black-holes.jpg)

_Showbiz_ by Muse - Alt rock/hard rock - I don't even like this one but my parents have all the Muse albums except _The 2nd Law_ for some reason so I've inherited them.

_Origin of Symmetry_ by Muse - Alt rock/prog rock - Quite a few standout singles here like _Plug In Baby_ and _New Born_/

_Absolution_ by Muse - Alt rock/prog rock - Don't like this one as much.

_Black Holes and Revelations_ by Muse - Alt rock/prog rock/hard rock - My favourite Muse album, just banger after banger, no notes.

_The Resistance_ by Muse - Alt rock/art rock - Eh, not my thing, but objectively a pretty solid album. _MK Ultra_ is a favourite.

_Drones_ by Muse - Alt rock/prog rock/hard rock - The first one I remember the release of. Relatively good.

_Simulation Theory_ by Muse - Electronic rock/new wave - Man, I want to hate this but I went to the stadium tour and it was the best gig I've ever seen so I can't. It's so earnest, it feels like kicking a puppy...![Danger Days](/assets/images/blog-pics/danger-days.jpg)

_Will of the People_ by Muse - Alt rock/glam rock - Matt says it's a "greatest hits album of new songs" and I can't help but agree - it's pure distilled Muse.

_I Brought You My Bullets, You Brought Me Your Love_ by My Chemical Romance - Post-hardcore/emo/punk rock - We're free of Muse but at what cost... I love how raw this is.

_Three Cheers for Sweet Revenge_ by My Chemical Romance - Emo/alt rock/pop-punk - Probably my least favourite MCR album, but it's still got many a banger.

_The Black Parade_ by My Chemical Romance - Emo/alt rock/pop-punk - Really solid, got to see the 2026 tour(!), one of the albums of all time, undeniably formative for a generation.

_Danger Days: The True Lives of the Fabulous Killjoys_ by My Chemical Romance - Alt rock/pop-punk/glam rock - I don't care what anyone says, this is the best MCR album ever and every single song on it makes me want to drive a car off a cliff. I also have the deluxe edition but I won't list it twice. Plus it's not actually on my CD shelf since it doesn't fit. ![The Fat of the Land](/assets/images/blog-pics/fat-of-land.jpg)

_The Downward Spiral_ by Nine Inch Nails - Industrial - There's a post somewhere about someone listening to this album and thinking the bin lorry outside was part of the music. I can see that. It's dope.

_Supernova_ by Nova Twins - Alt rock/punk rock - This album is good but I'm still on the hunt for _We Are The Girls_, which was better. Their new stuff is rubbish.

_The Fat of the Land_ by The Prodigy - Big beat/electropunk/techno - Another incredible album inherited from my mum. I adore it, it's great for workouts.

_Invaders Must Die_ by The Prodigy - Big beat/new rave - This is a bit more mainstream but goddamnit I still love it. _Omen_ is an all-timer.

_The Rise and Fall of a Midwest Princess_ by Chappell Roan - Synth-pop/alt-pop - I liked this before it was cool, OK? I've been obsessing over Chappell since the _Casual_ music video. I broke out of hospital to see Chappell at Reading. It was worth it.

_Scissor Sisters_ by Scissor Sisters - Glam rock/nu-disco - _Take Your Mama_ is a banger.![The Rise and Fall of a Midwest Princess](/assets/images/blog-pics/midwest-princess.png)

_Ta-Dah_ by Scissor Sisters - Glam rock/pop rock - _I Don't Feel Like Dancin'_ and _I Can't Decide_ are bangers.

_Night Work_ by Scissor Sisters - Alt rock/glam rock - I don't think I've even listened to this album. I don't know why I have so much Scissor Sisters.

_Kilroy Was Here_ by Styx - Pop rock - Absolutely awesome album, if only for what it could have been.

_Simply The Best_ by Tina Turner - Pop/rock - Tina Turner is totally underrated in the rock afficionado crowd and everyone should watch Mad Max: Thunderdome.

_Under the Crooked Moon_ by The Hot Puppies - Rock(?) - Picked up in a charity shop. It's fine.

_Venom_ by Wargasm - Nu metal/synth-punk - Sick album but Wargasm is best experienced live, I highly recommend their shows.

_Highlights from Jeff Wayne's Musical Version of the War of the Worlds_ by Jeff Wane et al - Prog rock - An album I was raised on, and my dad before me. Genuinely incredible experience to listen to, and if you can find a video of the original musical watch that too. Don't bother with the new versions. Jeff, if you're reading this, time to let it die.

_Once in a Lifetime: 40 Classic New Wave Hits_ by various artists - what do you think - I just like new wave OK.
