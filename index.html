About the webmaster

My name, as far as you're concerned, is Blue, and I'm a twenty-something year old Brit with a passion for biosciences and endurance sports. I like to outsorce my pronouns to the people who actually have to use them (A.K.A. not me) so go nuts. Media-wise, I'm a fan of anything that can even slightly be considered sci-fi or horror, ideally some combination of the two, but I'm pretty genre-fluid. I love rock music, making/mutilating my own clothes and scaring adult men in the gym.

I first discovered webdev as a hobby when I got covid really badly, had a terrible fever and designed and built most of a website before regaining consciousness again. I've been maintaining it on and off since then. This latest iteration is hopefully somewhat responsive, making it immediately better than the previous version. It's also a darn sight more accessible than my old one. Still ugly though. I mean, look. I can't even get these webring links to sit in a nice orderly way.
